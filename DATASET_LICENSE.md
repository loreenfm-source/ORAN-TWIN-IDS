Twinning Dataset**:

> L. Bonati, R. Shirkhani, C. Fiandrino, S. Maxenti, S. D'Oro, M. Polese and
> T. Melodia, "Twinning commercial network traces on experimental Open RAN
> platforms," in *Proceedings of the 30th Annual International Conference on
> Mobile Computing and Networking (MobiCom)*, 2024, pp. 1914–1921.
